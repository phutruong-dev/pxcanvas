## Why Personalised Pricing

### Why we don't publish rates

A flat rate can't read your trip. Group size, length of stay, and the time of year change what the villa costs to run. We'd rather write the number for you than post one that misses by half.

#### `Reason 1` · Quote includes everything

Housekeeping, taxes, pool heating, welcome basket — already in the number we send.

#### `Reason 2` · No fees we didn't show you

What's in the quote is the price.

#### `Reason 3` · Long stays get a fairer price

A week and a fortnight don't pay the same per night. A flat rate can't do that.
