When the doors open and close.

#### `Check-in` · From 3pm

On the day of arrival.

#### `Check-out` · By 11am

On the day of departure.

#### `Late check-out` · On request

Subject to the next booking. Ask us 24 hours in advance and we'll do our best.

---

## Quiet Hours

### Between 10pm and 8am

Music indoors and outdoors is fine through the day. Outdoor speakers come down to indoor levels after 10pm, out of respect for the neighbours.

---

## Smoking

### No smoking indoors

This includes vapes and e-cigarettes.

Outdoor terraces are fine. Please use the ashtrays provided.

A €500 cleaning fee applies if indoor smoking is detected.

---

## Events & Parties

### Case-by-case

Quiet dinners, family celebrations, and gatherings of registered guests are welcome.

Larger events — birthdays with extra attendees, photo shoots, weddings, parties — need written approval and a separate fee.

Mention the occasion when you inquire, and we'll work out the details together.

---

## Children & Pool Safety

### Welcome, with supervision

Children of all ages are welcome.

The villa is private but not childproof. Both pools and the hot tub are accessible — adult supervision is required at all times.

We can arrange pool covers, gates, and additional childcare on request. Ask us before you arrive.

---

## Pets

### Small, well-behaved pets on request

Tell us the breed and size when you inquire. Some areas of the villa — the wine cellar and bedrooms with linens not yet changed — are off-limits.

A €200 additional cleaning fee applies for pet stays.

---

## Damage & Security Deposit

### €2,000 refundable

A refundable security deposit is held against damage.

The deposit is returned in full within 7 days of check-out, less the cost of any documented damage.

Normal wear is expected and not deducted.

---

## Linens & Towels

### Changed daily

Bed linens and towels are provided and changed daily.

Pool towels are stored by the outdoor pool — please use those rather than bath towels for swimming.

---

## Maximum Occupancy

### Twelve registered guests

The villa sleeps twelve registered guests. Additional overnight guests are not permitted without prior approval.

Day visitors are welcome — please let us know if you're expecting more than four.

---

## Lost Keys & Access

### Smart locks at entry

Codes are provided on arrival.

If a physical key or fob is lost, a replacement fee of €50 per item applies.

---

## Final Cleaning

### Included in your quote

We ask only that dishes are placed in the dishwasher and that rubbish is bagged before check-out — the team handles the rest.

---

## Force Majeure

### Events outside our control

Severe weather, natural disasters, government restrictions — handled on a case-by-case basis with goodwill on both sides.
