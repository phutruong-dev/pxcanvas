## Inline FAQ

### Five quick answers

For visitors who'd rather scan before submitting.

#### How much does it cost?

Rates depend on season, length of stay, and group size. We send a personalised quote within 24 hours of your inquiry — it includes housekeeping, taxes, pool heating, and the welcome basket.

#### What's the minimum stay?

Three nights in low season (Nov–Mar), five in mid season, seven in peak (Jul–Aug) and over holidays.

#### How do I confirm a booking?

Once you accept the quote, a 30% deposit confirms your dates. The balance is due 30 days before check-in.

#### Can I cancel?

Free cancellation up to 60 days before check-in. 50% refund between 30 and 60 days. Inside 30 days, the booking is non-refundable.

#### Are children welcome?

Yes — cribs, high chairs, and baby gear are available on request. Tell us ages in the form and we'll prepare.
