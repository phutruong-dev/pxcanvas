## The Neighbourhood

### A quiet street, above the harbour

Bruna Bušića is residential — neighbours, a few small shops, a bakery on the corner.

You walk down to the Old Town in eight minutes; you walk back up in twelve. The street holds its quiet through the day and longer into the night than the harbour does.

## Day Trips

### Around the coast

Six trips worth a day. We'll book the boat or the driver when you ask.

#### `Half day` · Lokrum island

Short boat ride from the harbour, peacocks, a swim in a saltwater lake.

#### `Full day` · Elaphiti islands

Three islands, three lunches, the sea between.

#### `Full day` · Mljet National Park

Pine forests, two saltwater lakes, kayaks for hire.

#### `Half day` · Konavle wine country

Three producers inland, lunch in a stone konoba.

#### `Full day` · Kotor, Montenegro

Fjord-shaped bay, a walled town, a longer drive.

#### `Long day` · Korčula

Birthplace of Marco Polo, white wines, narrow streets.
