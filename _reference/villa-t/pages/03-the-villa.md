## Layout

### Five floors, easy to find quiet

Rooms spread across five floors, connected by an elevator. The wellness floor sits at the bottom; the bedrooms and views climb.

When the kids are awake on floor one, the master suite on floor three is still asleep.

#### `Lower floor` · Wellness

Indoor heated pool with swim-jet, infrared sauna, hot tub jacuzzi, chill-out room with bar and snooker table.

#### `Ground floor` · Living & outdoor

Main kitchen and dining, living room, outdoor kitchen with barbecue, outdoor heated infinity pool, dining terrace for twelve.

#### `Floor 1` · Bedrooms

Master Suite (Sea View) and two standard rooms.

#### `Floor 2` · Bedrooms

Master Suite (Old Town View) and two standard rooms.

#### `Roof` · Sunset deck

Quiet, lounge chairs, the whole coast at the end of the day.
