## What We Collect

### Only what we need

To reply to your inquiry, manage your booking, and improve our website.

Full name, email address, phone number (optional).

#### `From bookings` · Stay details

Dates of stay, group size, information you provide in your message.

#### `Payment` · Processed by our payment provider

We do not store card details.

#### `Legal` · ID documents

Required by Croatian law for confirmed bookings only.

IP address, rough geographic location, browser type, device, pages visited, referrer.
