## Booking & Payment

### Getting from inquiry to confirmed

The transactional bits.

#### How do I check availability?

Use the form on our Check Availability page. Send your dates and group size; we reply within 24 hours with availability and a personalised quote.

#### How much does Villa T cost?

Rates depend on the season, length of stay, group size, and the services you choose. We send a personalised quote within 24 hours. Quotes include daily housekeeping, taxes, pool heating, Wi-Fi, parking, and the welcome basket.

#### What's the minimum stay?

Three nights in low season (November–March), four in shoulder (April, May, October), five in mid (June, September), and seven in peak (July, August) and over holidays.

#### How much is the deposit?

A 30% deposit confirms your booking. The balance is due 30 days before check-in.

#### Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer or major credit cards. We send a secure payment link with your quote.

#### What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation more than 60 days before check-in (less a processing fee). 50% refund between 30 and 60 days. Non-refundable within 30 days. Full policy on the cancellation page.

## The Villa

### Space, layout, who fits

What the villa actually holds.

#### How many guests can stay at Villa T?

Up to twelve, in six ensuite bedrooms — two master suites and four standard rooms. Every bedroom has its own bathroom.

#### Are children welcome?

Yes. Cribs, high chairs, and baby gear are available on request. Tell us ages in your inquiry and we'll prepare.

#### Are pets allowed?

Case-by-case. Small, well-behaved pets are welcome on request — let us know the breed and size when you inquire.

#### Is the villa accessible?

The villa has an elevator across all five floors. The entry level offers step-free access and a wheelchair-friendly bathroom.

#### Is there an elevator?

Yes — across all five floors.

## Services

### Chef, transfers, add-ons

What we can arrange alongside the stay.

#### Is breakfast included?

The welcome basket is included; a daily breakfast service is an add-on. Tell us which style — continental, English, American, regional, vegetarian, vegan — and we'll include it in your quote.

#### Can you arrange a private chef?

Yes. Our chef writes a seasonal menu and plates it on the terrace or downstairs. Tick the chef box on the form and we'll include it in your quote.

#### Do you offer airport transfers?

Yes. Meet-and-greet at Dubrovnik (DBV), 25 minutes by car. We can also arrange returns and inter-city transfers.

#### Can we host an event or party?

Case-by-case. Quiet dinners are fine; larger gatherings need approval and a separate fee. Mention the occasion in your inquiry.

## Location & Arrival

### Getting here, getting around

The practical geography.

#### How far is the Old Town?

Eight minutes on foot to Pile Gate. Three minutes by car.

#### Where is the nearest beach?

Banje Beach is 12 minutes on foot. Sveti Jakov and Lapad have alternatives, all within a short drive.

#### How do we get from the airport?

Dubrovnik Airport (DBV) is 25 minutes by car. We arrange a meet-and-greet transfer on request.

#### Is there parking?

Free on-site parking for two cars.

## Stay Rules

### House rules, in brief

What's allowed, what's not.

#### What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in from 3pm. Check-out by 11am. Late check-out on request, subject to the next booking.

#### Is smoking allowed?

No smoking indoors. Outdoor terraces are fine.

#### Will there be other guests in the villa?

No — Villa T is yours for the stay. No shared check-in, no other parties.
