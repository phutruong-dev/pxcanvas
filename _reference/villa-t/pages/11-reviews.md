## Featured Story

### A week, six bedrooms, twelve guests

A longer review from a multi-generational stay.

#### The Marchetti family, Milan — `Family · September 2025`

> We booked Villa T for my parents' fortieth anniversary. We came as fourteen people across three generations — kids, grandparents, a few cousins. The villa held all of us without ever feeling crowded.
>
> Each family took a floor, the kids took the indoor pool, the grandparents claimed the master with the balcony. Dinner on the terrace, lit by the harbour, with the chef plating four courses — that's the photo we'll keep.
>
> The team replied to every message in the same window. We didn't think about a single transfer, grocery run, or chef booking — they handled it. Came home rested, which we hadn't expected.

## Recent Stays

### From the last twelve months

A selection of stays across personas and seasons.

#### Sarah & Tom, London — `Couples · June 2025`

> Five floors and six bedrooms — and a host who answered WhatsApp before we'd finished typing. Came for a wedding-adjacent week. Left planning the next one.

#### The Berger family, Munich — `Family · April 2025`

> Brought two kids, the grandparents, and a long list of requests. The team handled all of it. The indoor pool in April was the surprise win — heated, big enough to actually swim.

#### Mark & David, New York — `Couples · September 2024`

> We've stayed in plenty of villas. This is the first one where dinner felt like home. The chef night is worth the trip alone.

#### Hannah & friends, Stockholm — `Celebration · July 2025`

> Came as a group of ten for a fortieth birthday. The outdoor terrace is built for it — long table, sea view, the wine cellar nearby. The chef wrote a menu around the birthday girl's allergies without us asking twice.

#### A leadership team, Amsterdam — `Corporate retreat · October 2024`

> Booked Villa T for a leadership offsite. Wi-Fi handled video calls for six days, the outdoor table became our board table, and the chef plated lunch around our sessions. One invoice for the lot.

#### The Lefèvre family, Paris — `Family · May 2025`

> The walking distance to the Old Town is accurate — eight minutes down, twelve back. We did the walk daily without complaint. The villa was the right place to come home to.

#### Anna & Mikel, Madrid — `Honeymoon · June 2025`

> The master suite with the hot-tub bath is everything the photos suggest. The breakfast tray on the balcony every morning was the small detail that made the week.

#### Olivia & friends, Manchester — `Friends · February 2025`

> Brought eight friends for a long weekend. The indoor pool, the snooker room, the wine cellar — the villa knew what to do with a group like ours. Quiet ending in the sauna, every night.

#### Daniel & Mia, Copenhagen — `Couples · January 2025`

> Three things stood out: the bathroom floor heating in January, the chef's bread, and the host's reply speed. All of them surprised us. Will book again.
