## The Private Chef

### A seasonal menu, plated where you'd like

Our chef writes a menu around what's on the boats, in the gardens, in the markets.

Four courses on the terrace, or a long lunch downstairs by the cellar. Allergies and diets handled — tell us when you book.

The menu changes through the year. Spring leans on artichoke and lamb; summer turns to fish and tomatoes; autumn brings truffles from inland; winter slows down with braises and cellar wines.
