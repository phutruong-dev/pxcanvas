# Villa T — Cancellation Policy

## Hero

### Cancellation policy

Plans change. Here's how we handle it, in plain words.

---

## Refund Windows

### The amount refunded depends on timing

How close to check-in you cancel decides the refund.

#### `More than 60 days` · Full refund

Less a 5% processing fee.

#### `30 – 60 days` · 50% refund

Half of total held against the booking.

#### `Under 30 days` · Non-refundable

The room is unlikely to re-book at short notice.

---

## How to Cancel

### Email us with your booking reference

Email info@villatdubrovnik.com with your booking reference and the dates you'd like to cancel.

We confirm the cancellation in writing within 24 hours. Refunds are processed within 7 business days of confirmation, via the original payment method.

---

## Date Changes

### Move the dates rather than cancel

If your plans change but you'd still like to stay, ask us to move your dates rather than cancelling.

Date changes within the same season are usually free, subject to availability. Moving to a higher-priced season may attract a difference; moving to a lower-priced season does not generate a refund of the difference.

Date changes must be requested at least 30 days before the original check-in.

---

## Force Majeure

### Events outside the control of either party

Severe weather, natural disasters, government restrictions, airline collapse, war, public-health emergencies — handled on a case-by-case basis.

Where travel becomes legally impossible from your point of origin to Dubrovnik for the dates of your booking, we will offer either a full refund or a date change at no extra cost, at your discretion.

Documentation of the relevant restriction may be requested.

---

## Cancellation by Us

### A full refund, always

In the rare event we need to cancel a confirmed booking (extreme weather damage to the villa, for example), you receive a full refund of all amounts paid.

Where possible, we will offer an alternative date or, if you prefer, a credit for a future stay.

---

## Travel Insurance

### Strongly recommended

We recommend comprehensive travel insurance for every booking, including coverage for cancellation, illness, and travel disruption.

Travel insurance is not a substitute for our cancellation policy, but it covers situations beyond our policy — including personal illness, family emergencies, and changes of plan unrelated to force majeure.

---

## Refund Timing

### Seven business days, usually faster

Refunds are processed within 7 business days of cancellation confirmation.

Card refunds may take an additional 3 to 10 business days to appear, depending on your bank.

Bank transfer refunds are typically faster but depend on receiving bank timing.
