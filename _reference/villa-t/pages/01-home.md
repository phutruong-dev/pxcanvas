## Location Preview — Around the Villa

### Eight minutes from the walls

Quiet residential street above the Old Town, with the airport 25 minutes by car.

Villa T sits above the Old Town, on a quiet residential street. You walk down for dinner, the cathedral bells, the harbour at dusk — and walk back to a heated pool. The airport is 25 minutes by car. The island of Lokrum is a short boat ride from the harbour.

#### `8 min on foot` · Old Town (Pile Gate)

The walls hit the harbour just past the gate.

#### `12 min on foot` · Banje Beach

North along the waterline, sand and a beach club.

#### `10 min on foot` · Mount Srđ cable car

The whole coast from up top.
