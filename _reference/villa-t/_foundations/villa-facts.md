## 8. Booking process

1. Guest submits inquiry → form on `/check-availability` (or "Request a Quote").
2. We reply within 24 hours with a personalised quote + availability.
3. Quote held free for 48 hours.
4. Guest pays 30% deposit → booking confirmed.
5. Final balance 30 days before check-in.
6. Welcome call 7 days before arrival to plan add-ons.
7. Check-in 3pm. Check-out 11am (late check-out on request).
